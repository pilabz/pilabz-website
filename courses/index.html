COURSE OUTLINE
This 4 week online program is crafted to equip students with a critical, industry-relevant skill: the ability to design and analyze electric motors using modern simulation software. The curriculum will cover essential topics, including:

• Fundamentals of Motor Components and Physics: A review of the core principles of electric motors and vehicle dynamics.
• Introduction to Finite Element Methods (FEM): The theoretical foundation for advanced simulation.
• Hands-on PiCADS-Motor Training: Practical instruction on using the software, including its internal workings and setup.
• Multi-Physics Simulation and output interpretation: An in-depth look at the interconnected thermal and structural concepts behind motor models.
