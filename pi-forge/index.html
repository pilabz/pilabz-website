Why Pi-Forge?

The modern engineering ecosystem

Pi-Forge brings product design, computation, simulation and digital validation into one environment so teams can move from geometry to a decision without stitching disconnected tools together.

Design

  • Build engineering geometry

    Create parts, assemblies and simulation-ready models for development and validation.

  • Drive models with parameters

    Change dimensions and constraints before meshing or running a study.

  • Inspect regions and assemblies

    Review geometry and flow volumes in interactive 3D views.

Simulate

  • Evaluate behaviour digitally

    Study structural, thermal, flow, electrical and coupled response under load.

  • Run connected studies

    Move between single-domain and multi-domain simulations in one workflow.

  • Validate before you build

    Find issues early and cut repeated physical prototypes.

Analyse

  • Review results

    Read field data, plots and performance trends with a clear technical view.

  • Compare configurations

    See how geometry and input changes move the result.

  • Support the next decision

    Use analysis to confirm assumptions before the next design step.

Integrate

  • Connect design and simulation

    Keep geometry, mesh, solve, visualisation and reporting in one place.

  • Cut hand-offs

    Reduce file bouncing, duplicate setup and manual preprocessing.

  • Keep the project together

    Modelling, analysis and reports stay in a shared workspace.

Optimise

  • Iterate with intent

    Adjust geometry, materials and operating conditions to improve the result.

  • Weigh the trade-offs

    Balance efficiency, cost, manufacturability and reliability.

  • Improve systematically

    Use structured studies to reach a stronger design, not a lucky one.
